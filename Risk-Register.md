# Security Risk Register

## Project Background

This assessment examines cybersecurity risks at Oakridge Financial Services, a fictional organization that handles sensitive customer financial information.

**Disclaimer:** This is an educational simulation. The findings and risk ratings are hypothetical and are not based on a real company audit.

## R-001: Weak and Reused Passwords

**Asset:** Employee accounts and sensitive customer information

**Vulnerability:** Employees use weak passwords and reuse passwords across multiple accounts.

**Threat:** An attacker could obtain stolen credentials and attempt to access company systems through credential stuffing or other unauthorized login attempts.

**Potential impact:** If an attacker successfully gains access, customer information could be exposed, identities could be stolen, and the organization could experience financial or reputational harm.

### Preliminary Risk Rating

- Likelihood: 3/3 — High
- Impact: 3/3 — High
- Overall risk score: 9/9 — High

These are provisional ratings based on the fictional scenario and require additional evidence to validate.

### My Reasoning

I prioritized weak and reused passwords because people often choose passwords that are familiar and easy to remember. Reusing passwords may seem convenient, but it can create a domino effect if credentials from one breached account are also used for other accounts.

For a company that handles sensitive financial information, this could lead to serious consequences, including unauthorized access, identity theft, and exposure of customer information.

### Recommended Security Controls

1. Require unique, strong passwords for company accounts.
2. Implement multifactor authentication (MFA), preferably phishing-resistant methods.
3. Provide an approved password manager.
4. Check for compromised passwords and monitor suspicious sign-in activity.
5. Train employees on password security and credential phishing.

### Evidence Needed

- Current password policy
- MFA enrollment and enforcement status
- Authentication and suspicious-login records
- Results of authorized compromised-credential checks
